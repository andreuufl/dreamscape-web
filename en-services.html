Services

Technical production for your event.

Sound, lighting, staging and LED screens with a technician available (quoted separately), anywhere in Spain.

We supply and operate the technical side of your event: sound, lighting, staging and LED screens, delivered, installed, tested and, if you add a technician, run by our own. One point of contact from the first call to the load-out.

01

Sound

Line arrays and PA sized to the room and guest count, wireless mics, DJ and live-band backline, with a sound engineer on site throughout.

02

Lighting

Moving heads, architectural and ambient lighting, DMX control from a single desk, with IP65 fixtures for outdoor events.

03

Staging & structures

Stages, truss and rigging calculated for each venue, with plans and load-in times agreed with the venue.

04

LED screens & video

LED walls, projection and live camera mix for presentations, congresses and hybrid events.

Event types

  • Corporate events: product launches, galas, conventions and team events.
  • Congresses and trade shows: plenary rooms, breakouts, simultaneous interpretation set-ups and live streaming.
  • Weddings and celebrations: ceremony sound, dance-floor lighting and outdoor set-ups in venues and country estates. See destination weddings in Spain.
  • Concerts and shows: stage, backline, PA and lighting design.

How we work

  1. You send date, venue and guest count; if needed we review or visit the space.
  2. We send a technical proposal and quote, usually within 24 hours.
  3. We install and test with time to spare before guests arrive.
  4. A technician (quoted separately) stays at the desk for the whole event, with backup equipment.
  5. We load out and leave the space as we found it.

Where we work

Based in Barcelona, with a regular team in Madrid and Valencia. We travel with our own equipment and technicians anywhere in Spain, including the Balearic and Canary Islands. Our rider simulator (in Spanish) gives you an indicative equipment list for your event.

Tell us about your event

Date, venue and guest count are enough. We reply with a technical proposal and quote, normally within 24 hours.
