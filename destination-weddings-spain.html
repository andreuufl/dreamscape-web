Frequently asked questions

What does destination wedding sound and lighting production in Spain include?

Wireless microphones for the ceremony, sound for the reception and party, decorative and architectural lighting for the venue, and a dedicated technician on-site for the whole day, so you don't have to think about anything technical.

Can you set up sound and lighting at outdoor estates and gardens in Spain?

Yes, we regularly work at fincas, gardens and other open-air venues across Spain. We use IP65-rated equipment built to handle the outdoors without compromising the technical or visual result.

Do you work with our wedding planner, or only directly with the couple?

Both. We coordinate timing, load-in and technical rehearsals directly with your wedding planner, or work with you directly if you don't have one, so the day runs exactly as planned.

How far in advance should we book?

We recommend getting in touch at least 2-3 months ahead, and earlier if your wedding falls in peak season (spring-summer in Spain), to guarantee equipment and technician availability on your exact date.

Can we get a quote before travelling to Spain to see the venue?

Yes. Most of our destination wedding clients get their full technical proposal and quote remotely, over email or a video call, based on photos, floor plans and the venue's own technical information. A site visit isn't required to get started.
