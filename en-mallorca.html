We travel to Mallorca with our own equipment and technicians, and plan the sea freight from the first quote. Weddings, destination weddings, corporate events and conferences in hotels, estates and outdoor venues across the island.

Typical venues in Mallorca

  • Palma Congress Centre and venues around the bay.
  • Inland fincas and possessions for weddings and celebrations.
  • Coastal hotels and resorts for corporate events and destination weddings.
  • Coves, terraces and outdoor spaces, with venue permits.

What we take into account in Mallorca

  • Island logistics: equipment travels by ferry with advance booking. We allow extra build days and a buffer for delays.
  • High season (summer): ferries, transport and estates fill up early. Book ahead.
  • Outdoor events: wind and humidity; we use protected equipment and a plan B.

What our service includes

01

Professional sound

PA and wireless microphones sized to the audience and the acoustics, with a sound technician (quoted separately) for the whole event.

02

Lighting and DMX

Moving heads, architectural and ambient lighting, with DMX control from the booth.

03

Stages and structures

Platforms, truss and rigging calculated for each venue, with plans and load-in times coordinated in advance.

04

LED screens and video

LED screens, projection and live production for presentations, conferences and streaming.

How we work

  1. You tell us the date, venue and audience size, and we visit or review the space if needed.
  2. We send a technical proposal and quote, usually within 24 hours.
  3. We build and test with time to spare before guests arrive.
  4. A technician in the booth for the whole event (quoted separately), with backup equipment.
  5. We load out and leave the venue as we found it.

Frequently asked questions about events in Mallorca

How does the equipment get to Mallorca?

We ship it by ferry with advance booking, together with the technicians. The quote includes transport and the build days required.

Do you do destination weddings in Mallorca?

Yes. We coordinate with wedding planners and venues so the technical side (sound, lighting, ceremony and party) is fully settled, in English too if needed.

Does the technician stay for the whole event?

Yes. The technician can stay in the booth for the whole event with backup equipment, not just for the build. The technician is not included in the equipment price: it is quoted separately.

How can I get a quote for my event in Mallorca?

Set up your event in the simulator to see the suggested equipment, or message us on WhatsApp with the date, venue and number of guests. We usually reply within 24 hours.

We also work in Barcelona and Valencia, and travel to other parts of Spain depending on the project.
